How we work

We act as your electrical studies office. Sign-off and certification of the project rest with the licensed engineer appointed by you or your engineering firm.

  1. 1

    Enquiry and proposal

    Tell us about the project: voltage, capacity, system operator and stage. We send a proposal with scope, price and a timeline set according to that scope.

  2. 2

    Input data

    We give you an exact list of what we need: system operator requirements, single-line diagram and equipment data. No back and forth.

  3. 3

    Models and criteria

    We build the models in DIgSILENT PowerFactory, PSCAD, PSS/E or ETAP, as the system operator requires, following IEC, Spanish regulations and VDE-AR-N. You review and accept the models and calculation criteria.

  4. 4

    Report ready for sign-off

    A traceable report with assumptions, results and conclusions, delivered within 4 weeks once all parameters are defined and the models and calculations are accepted.

  5. 5

    Follow-up

    We answer system operator queries on the study, under the terms agreed in the proposal.
