How we work

We act as your electrical studies office. Sign-off and certification of the project rest with the licensed engineer appointed by you or your engineering firm.

  1. 1

    Input data

    System operator requirements, plant and equipment data. We tell you exactly what is needed.

  2. 2

    Model and calculation

    We model the grid and the installation and run the studies in industry-standard software.

  3. 3

    Technical report

    A traceable calculation report with assumptions, results and conclusions, ready for review and sign-off.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    We answer system operator queries and update the study if the project changes.
