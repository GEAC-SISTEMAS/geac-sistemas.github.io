3. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Fonts are loaded from our own server; no connections are made to Google Fonts or other third parties.

If you contact us, we process your details (name, email, phone number, company and the content of your enquiry) to answer it and, where relevant, prepare a proposal. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the data once it is no longer needed, unless we are legally required to keep it.

5. Newsletter

If you ask to receive the newsletter, we use your email address only to send it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by emailing info@geacengsol.de.

This website contains a plain link to our LinkedIn page. No data is sent to LinkedIn unless you click it; after that, LinkedIn's privacy policy applies.

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Arts. 15–21 GDPR), and may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.
